About InsightScholar

What is InsightScholar?

InsightScholar is school management and reporting software for Zimbabwean schools. It handles report cycles, mark and comment entry, HOD and head approval, PDF report cards, learner import and analytics.

Which schools is it for?

Zimbabwean schools running classes, streams and three terms a year. If your school is organised differently, tell us and we will show you how it fits.

Do we need to install anything?

No. InsightScholar runs in a web browser, so staff can use it on the phones and laptops they already have.

Is there a teacher app?

A teacher app with roll call and timetables is in development. Ask to be notified and we will tell you when it is ready.

Setting up

How do we get started?

Book a demo. After it, you register your school, we approve it, and your staff join with a private registration code.

Can we use our existing class lists?

Yes. Upload your class lists as CSV or Excel files. InsightScholar flags names that look like duplicates so you can confirm or merge them before they are added.

What does it cost?

Contact us for pricing for your school. We will go through it with you after your demo.

Marks, reports and data

Who can see learners' marks?

Access depends on each person's role. Teachers see the classes they teach, HODs see their department, and the head and admins see the whole school.

How do report cards reach parents?

Once the head approves a class, InsightScholar creates a PDF report card for every learner. You can print them or share them with parents.

Can a class be sent back for changes?

Yes. HODs and the head can return a class to the teacher with a note explaining what to fix. It comes back for review once it is updated.

Who we are
