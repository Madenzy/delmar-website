Heritage Prestige Program

Elevating school standards. Inspiring future aviators.

A program by Delmar Technologies (Pvt) Ltd

Our mission is to bring the full aviation ecosystem to the school through school management, mentorship, aviation experiences and recognised certificates, for less than the price of a single sheet of paper per learner.

Aviation in Zimbabwe was in pieces. This program connects it for learners.

Schools were asked to put the Heritage Based Curriculum into practice, but had no industry partner to show them how. The airport, the tower, pilots and training were never joined up for learners.

The Heritage Prestige Program brings them together for the first time, so every learner can connect to their heritage, their talent and a real industry.

Safety and child protection

These rules apply to every activity, every time.

  • All flights are run by licensed operators under CAAZ regulations
  • No learner flies without written parental consent
  • Parental consent forms for every activity
  • Supervised groups, with school staff present at all times
  • Full indemnity and a safety briefing before every excursion

Our school platform

What is InsightScholar?

InsightScholar is our school management and reporting software for Zimbabwean schools. It handles report cycles, mark and comment entry, approval by HODs and heads, PDF report cards, learner import and results by class and subject.

Which schools is it for?

Zimbabwean schools running classes, streams and three terms a year. If your school is organised differently, tell us and we will show you how it fits.

Do we need to install anything?

No. It runs in a web browser, so staff can use it on the phones and laptops they already have.

Is there a teacher app?

A teacher app with roll call and timetables is in development. Ask us and we will tell you when it is ready.

Setting up

How do we get started?

Arrange a meeting with us. After it, you register your school, we approve it, and your staff join with a private registration code.

Can we use our existing class lists?

Yes. Upload your class lists as CSV or Excel files. Names that look like duplicates are flagged so you can confirm or merge them before they are added.

What does it cost?

Contact us for pricing for your school. We will go through it with you after we meet.

Marks, reports and data

Who can see learners’ marks?

Access depends on each person’s role. Teachers see the classes they teach, HODs see their department, and the head and admins see the whole school.

How do report cards reach parents?

Once the head approves a class, a PDF report card is created for every learner. You can print them or share them with parents.

Can a class be sent back for changes?

Yes. HODs and the head can return a class to the teacher with a note explaining what to fix. It comes back for review once it is updated.

Heritage Prestige Program

What does a member school get?

The Delmar school platform, printed reports, support with HBC School Based Projects, Heritage Prestige School status and access to national aviation partners.

Who runs the flights?

All flights are run by licensed operators under CAAZ regulations.

Does a learner need permission to take part?

Yes. Every activity needs a parental consent form, and no learner flies without written parental consent. School staff are with every group.

Privacy policy

How Delmar Technologies collects, uses and protects information in InsightScholar and on this website.

Last updated 12 November 2026

Who we are

Delmar Technologies (Pvt) Ltd (“Delmar”, “we”) is a company registered in Zimbabwe, based in Greystone Park, Harare. We provide InsightScholar and other software to schools and organisations.

Information we handle

When a school uses InsightScholar, we handle information on the school’s behalf, including:

  • School details such as the school’s name, forms, streams and subjects.
  • Staff details such as names, contact details, roles and sign-in information.
  • Learner records such as names, classes, marks, grades and teacher comments.

When you contact us, we receive the details you choose to send, such as your name, school and phone number or email.

How we use it

We use this information to run InsightScholar for your school, to support your staff, to keep the service secure, and to reply to your enquiries. We do not sell personal information.

Who can see it

Inside InsightScholar, each person sees only what their role allows. Delmar staff access school data only when needed to provide or support the service.

Your rights

Schools, staff and parents can ask us what information we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where the law allows. We handle personal information in line with Zimbabwe’s Cyber and Data Protection Act.

Contact

For privacy questions, email info@delmartechnologies.co.zw or write to us at our Harare office.
